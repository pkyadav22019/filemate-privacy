FileMate Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

In short: FileMate works on your device. Your files are not uploaded to any server and we do not have user accounts. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which may collect limited device data (see section 6).

1. Who we are

FileMate (“the app”) is a PDF tools and document scanner app developed by Pawan Kumar. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit your files or personal information ourselves. The app has no sign-in and no servers that receive your files. The only data collected comes from the advertising service described in section 6.

Everything you do in FileMate (converting images to PDF, merging, splitting, compressing, scanning, and reading PDFs) is processed locally on your device.

3. Permissions the app uses

  • Camera: used only when you scan a document or take a photo to add to a PDF.
  • Photos and media: used only to let you choose images you want to convert to PDF.
  • File access: the app opens PDFs you pick with the system file picker, and saves PDFs to the location you choose. It does not browse your other files.

You can turn these permissions off at any time in your phone’s Settings. Some features will then stop working.

4. Where your files are stored

PDFs you create are stored in the app’s private storage on your device and are listed in the Files tab. When you use “Save to device”, a copy is saved to the folder you pick. These copies are yours and are not accessible to us.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your files or personal information. When you tap Share, the file is handed to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp or Gmail). That app’s own privacy policy applies from that point.

6. Ads and third-party components

FileMate shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device’s advertising ID, IP address, and general device and app-usage information to serve and measure ads, and may show personalized ads. This data is handled by Google, not by us. See how Google uses data from ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your phone’s Settings (Google → Ads).

Advertisers do not get access to your files.

On some devices, the document scanner may use components provided by Google Play services. These run on your device, and Google’s privacy policy applies to them.

7. Deleting your data

You can delete any file from the Files tab. Uninstalling the app removes all files stored in its private storage. Copies you saved elsewhere on your device must be deleted from there.

8. Children

FileMate is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

9. Changes to this policy

If we add features that send files to a server (for example, cloud-based conversion), we will update this policy before releasing them. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
